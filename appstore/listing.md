### Description (4000)

```
Defend the data core in a neon cyberpunk city. Every tower you build is two choices: an ELEMENT that decides what the attack does, and a WEAPON that decides what it can hit.

FIVE ELEMENTS, SIX WEAPONS, 30 TOWERS
• Fire burns, Water chills and freezes, Wood roots and poisons, Earth stuns and breaks armor, Metal pierces and crits.
• Cannon, Flak, Multi-Shot, Mortar, Chain and Sniper: hit the ground, the air, or both.
• Every combination is its own tower, from the Inferno Mortar to the Railgun.

THE ELEMENT CYCLE
Enemies have elements too. Water beats Fire, Fire beats Metal, Metal beats Wood, Wood beats Earth, Earth beats Water. Pick the right counter for +50% damage.

ELEMENT COMBOS
Mix elements on the same enemy to set off combos: Steam, Wildfire, Shatter, Corrosion and Rupture.

A NEW BATTLEFIELD EVERY WAVE
Each wave is fought on Mars, Ocean, Jungle, Canyon or Factory, boosting one element and weakening another. A lockdown encrypts most towers each wave, so you can't build the same thing every time.

TEN HEROES
From map 3, a hero joins the fight: move them anywhere and unleash four abilities each, from Orbital Strike to Time Lock. Each hero has an element, a passive, and their own story.

7 MAPS, 15 TO 40 WAVES EACH
Shielders, Medics, Ghosts that hide from your towers, Phasers that teleport, Burrowers that dig under the road. Every map ends with a boss with several phases: the Siege Colossus, the Bulwark, the Chimera and the Sky Leviathan.

SCORE AND DAILY CHALLENGE
Every run is scored on waves held, clearing speed and lives kept. Beat your best on each map, and play the Daily Challenge: the same map, hero and conditions for everyone that day.

MADE FOR TOUCH
Tap to preview a tower and its range, tap again to build. Upgrade or sell any time, even mid-wave. Speed up to 3×. Your run is saved between waves.

• Plays fully offline
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
• English, Español, 中文, Tiếng Việt
```
