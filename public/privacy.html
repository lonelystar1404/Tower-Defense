Privacy Policy

Neon Wardens · effective October 8, 2026 (online rooms added)

Neon Wardens does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Unless you join an online room (below), nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

What the game stores, and where

To remember your game, Neon Wardens saves a few things on your own device only:

  • which maps you have cleared, and your best score on each map;
  • a saved run between waves, so you can continue later;
  • your best Daily Challenge score for the day;
  • your settings: language, sound on or off, and the last hero you picked;
  • a random Member ID (like NW-7F3K-9QM2-X4TD), created the first time you play. It is not based on your name, account, or device, and it only leaves your device if you join an online room.

In a web browser this is kept in the browser's local storage. In the iOS app it is kept in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see it. If your device is backed up (for example with iCloud Backup), this data may be part of that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

To delete it, delete the app (iOS), or clear this site's data in your browser settings (web).

Online multiplayer rooms (optional)

If you create or join an online room, the game uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database and anonymous sign-in) to connect the players. While the room is open, Firebase holds:

  • the room code, and a random anonymous sign-in ID that Firebase creates for your device (no name, email, or account);
  • your Member ID and the hero you picked, so the other players in the room can see who is who;
  • your moves in the game (towers you build, upgrade, or sell, where you move your hero, abilities you use) and the game's state at the end of each wave, so every player's game stays the same.

Only people with the room code can see the room. When the host leaves or the game ends, the room and everything in it is deleted. We don't use this data for anything else, and we don't keep it. Like any online service, Google processes connection data such as IP addresses to run Firebase; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. Playing alone, or with friends on one device, never uses Firebase.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. Firebase is used only for online rooms.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data.
  • The game does not need an internet connection to play, except in online rooms. All fonts, art, and sounds are built in.

Sharing your Daily Challenge result

If you tap Copy result after a Daily Challenge, a short text with your score is copied to your clipboard. It stays on your device until you choose to paste it somewhere.

The website

The web version and these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses to keep its service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if leaderboards or accounts are added), this page will be updated before the change takes effect, with a new date at the top.
